How to use Solfer

Getting started

  1. Tap Enable audio. Browsers need a tap before they allow sound.
  2. Choose the key, time signature and part (S, A, T, B).
  3. Enter notes with the on-screen keys or your keyboard.
  4. Press Enter (or the | bar key) to commit the measure. It moves to the Measures list.

Writing a measure

Notes are d r m f s l t. Chromatic notes are de me fe si ta. Beats are separated by :, and the number of beats must be a multiple of the top number of the time signature (4 beats in 4/4).

:Separates beats
.Splits a beat into two eighth notes: d.r
,Splits an eighth into two sixteenths: d,r.m
-Sustain: holds the previous note for another beat
spaceRest
'Octave up, e.g. d'
₁ ₂One or two octaves down

Examples (4/4)

  • d:r:m:f : four quarter notes
  • d.r:m:f:s : two eighths, then three quarters
  • d:r:m:- : d, r, then m held for two beats
  • d,r.m:f:s:l : two sixteenths, an eighth, then three quarters

Octaves

The Next note octave buttons in the sidebar, or the ↑ / ↓ arrow keys, set the octave for the notes you enter next.

Keyboard shortcuts

d r m f s l tEnter a note
SpaceRest
: . , -Beat separator, eighth, sixteenth, sustain
BackspaceDelete the last note or symbol
EnterCommit the measure
↑ ↓Change octave

Saved measures

▶ plays a measure, edit loads it back into the editor (commit again to save your changes), and ✕ deletes it.

Exporting

Exporting to .WAV is available to paid users with an active subscription.

Not supported yet

Triplet notation. Beats can currently be split into eighths and sixteenths only.

Heads up: triplets aren't supported yet

Solfer can't enter or play triplets (three notes in the space of one beat) yet. For now, beats can be split into eighth and sixteenth notes only.

Tap the ? in the top bar at any time for the full guide.
